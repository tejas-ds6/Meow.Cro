Privacy information.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Information you provide

When you register or manage an account, the site stores your name, email address, mobile number, password hash, and saved delivery addresses. When you place an order, it stores the order items, amount, payment and delivery details, and status history. Custom requests and contact forms may include messages or a reference photo.

How information is used

Account information is used to sign you in, help you reset your password, display your order history, arrange delivery, and provide support. Passwords are stored as salted, computationally expensive hashes, not as readable passwords. Password-reset tokens are stored as hashes and expire. Account sessions use an HttpOnly, SameSite cookie. The studio order desk uses the same order database to update delivery and cancellation status.

Payments, service providers and email

UPI payments are processed by Razorpay; the site does not store payment-card credentials. Order and password-reset emails are delivered using the configured email provider. PostgreSQL stores account, address, order, payment-reference, and order-status information for the site. These providers process information as needed to provide their services.

Browser storage

The shopping bag and recent checkout receipt details are stored in your browser. The sign-in session token is held in an HttpOnly cookie and is not available to page scripts. Custom-request confirmation details are stored temporarily in browser session storage. You can clear local browser storage through your browser settings; doing so does not delete database records.

Retention and your choices

Account and order information is retained in the store’s PostgreSQL database so customers and the studio can manage order history and delivery. Saved addresses and passwords can be changed through your account. To ask about or request deletion of account information, contact meowcrosia@gmail.com; order records may need to be retained for customer service, payment, or accounting purposes.

Changes and questions

This notice may be updated as the site’s features and practices change. For privacy questions, contact meowcrosia@gmail.com.

This plain-language notice describes the current site functionality and is not legal advice.
