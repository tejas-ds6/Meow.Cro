Privacy information.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Information you provide

When you place an order, ask about a custom piece, or contact the studio, the site asks for information needed to respond or arrange the requested order, such as your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and message. Custom requests may include a reference photo. The optional star-rating form submits only a rating and does not ask for your name or written review.

How information is used

Submitted details are used to review and respond to requests, process order enquiries, arrange delivery, and provide customer support. This site’s checkout is a demo flow and does not collect payment-card details or process a payment.

Service providers and email

Form submissions are sent to the studio by email using Resend, an email delivery provider. Information in messages is therefore processed by the studio’s email systems and the delivery provider to deliver those messages. Avoid including payment-card details or other highly sensitive information in forms.

Browser storage

The shopping bag and recent checkout details are stored in your browser so the site can maintain your session and show an order confirmation. Custom-request confirmation details are stored temporarily in browser session storage. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings.

Retention and your choices

The website does not currently provide customer accounts or an order-management database. Messages may remain in the studio’s email inbox and provider systems according to their retention practices. To ask about a message you sent or request its deletion from the studio’s inbox, email meowcrosia@gmail.com.

Changes and questions

This notice may be updated as the site’s features and practices change. For privacy questions, contact meowcrosia@gmail.com.

This plain-language notice describes the current site functionality and is not legal advice.
