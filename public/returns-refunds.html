A little note about changes

Returns & refunds.

Draft for studio review — this is not a finalized policy.

Please review before publishing as final policy. The studio has not yet confirmed these proposed terms. Contact us if you need help with an order.

Handmade pieces

Each item is made by hand and may have small variations from the photographs. These natural differences are part of handmade work and are not, on their own, a defect.

Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged or differs from the order you confirmed, contact orders@meowcro.in with your order number and clear photos as soon as reasonably possible. Please wait for the studio’s instructions before sending anything back.

Custom and personalized orders

Because custom pieces are made to an individual request, they may not be eligible for a change-of-mind return. This proposed exception would not remove the need to resolve an item that arrives damaged or materially differs from what the studio confirmed.

Cancellations and refunds

Contact the studio promptly if you need to cancel. A cancellation may not be possible once handmade work has started or an order has been dispatched. If a refund is approved, the studio will confirm the amount and method directly with you.

Before this draft is adopted, the studio should confirm reporting time limits, return shipping responsibility, cancellation cutoffs, and refund processing timelines.
