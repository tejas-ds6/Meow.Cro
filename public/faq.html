A few little answers

Frequently asked things.

Still wondering? Send us a note.

Are your pieces handmade?

Yes. Each piece is crocheted by hand, so small variations in shape, color, or finish are part of its handmade character.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag and submit the checkout form. This website does not collect payment; the studio will email you to confirm availability, delivery details, and how to pay.

What are the shipping charges?

Shipping is ₹29 for order totals of ₹599 or less, and free for totals above ₹599. The studio will confirm whether delivery is available to your address and share timing after reviewing your order. Read the shipping information.

Can I request a custom piece?

Yes. Share your idea, preferred colors, budget, and timing on our custom orders page. We’ll confirm whether it’s possible before accepting the request.

Can I cancel or return an order?

Please contact us as soon as possible. Handmade or custom work may already be underway. Our returns information is currently a draft and needs the studio’s final approval before it is treated as policy. View the draft.

Where can I track my order?

Live tracking isn’t available on the website yet. The studio will share delivery updates directly when they are available. Order update information.

How can I reach the studio?

Email meowcrosia@gmail.com or use the contact form.
